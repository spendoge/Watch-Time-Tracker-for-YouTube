What it does

The tracker sits above the recommended videos on a watch page, and beside the player on Shorts. It starts counting when you install it.

  • Live watch time

    Your last 24 hours, counting second by second, split between videos and Shorts.

  • Week, month and year

    Running totals for the past week, month and year.

  • Most watched channels

    Your top channels by total watch time, with gold, silver and bronze for the first three.

  • A graph of every day

    Watch time by day, month or year, with videos and Shorts stacked.

  • Works on Shorts

    It follows the Shorts player and can shrink to a small pill.

Your data stays yours

Your watch time is stored on your own device and is never sent to the developer.

  • Optional backup

    Sign in with Google to keep a copy in your own Google Drive, so it survives reinstalling and is shared between your devices.

  • Backup files

    Export your data to a file, or import one, at any time. No account needed.

  • Delete everything

    One button erases your watch time, including the copy in your Google Drive if sync is on.
